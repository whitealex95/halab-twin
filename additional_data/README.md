# Additional reference photographs

These three original photographs supplement the HaLab RGB-D capture. They were taken separately after the capture and have no calibrated poses or depth images.

- `WhiteCabinet_view1.jpg`: open white cabinet, documenting three interior shelves and the door construction. Shelf heights are estimated by proportion within the captured cabinet dimensions.
- `Desk_view1.jpg`, `Desk_view2.jpg`: two views of the desk normally concealed behind the desk divider. The desk was moved for these photos. Its reconstructed placement follows the captured room layout; tabletop objects in these photos are omitted.

The files are preserved byte-for-byte. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains their checksums. These images are packaged separately as `halab-additional-data.zip`; the original 983-file RGB-D archive remains unchanged.
